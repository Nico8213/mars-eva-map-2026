# Video script (30 seconds)

One version, matched to the live page as checked on 15 Nov 2026. Each "Say" line is narration; each "Screen" line is what the recording shows. Word counts cover the "Say" lines only.

## What the page shows today

- Three layers, each with an opacity slider and a "Data info" button: Slope, Hillshade, Optical (CTX 6 m).
- Points of interest: Cliff A, Plateau B, Crater C, Cone D. A popup shows type, source and evidence. The points were selected by eye from the hillshade. They are not CRISM detections.
- Data cards: product, resolution, license, acquisition date, known issues, and the decision the layer affects.
- The status line reads "Data loaded (static snapshot)".
- Routes and validation results: **only film these scenes if they are live on the page when you record.**

## Rules for the recording

- Do not say or show a spectral layer. There is none. Spectral data (CRISM) is a reference only and does not set any route or point.
- Do not show JMARS or any screenshot from it.
- Do not say "live data" or "real-time". The page is a static snapshot.
- Say a route is checked against the Perseverance path only if the validation report exists and its numbers are on the page. Use the report's wording, whatever the result.
- Any AI-generated footage (for example a rotating Mars globe) carries the caption "Illustration - not project data" and is listed in the AI use statement.
- Keep the status line "Data loaded (static snapshot)" in frame at least once.

## Script

1. **Say:** Planning a walk on Mars starts with one question: what can we trust?
   **Screen:** Open the site. Hold on the full map with the three layers on.
2. **Say:** Martian Map shows Jezero Crater from NASA orbital data.
   **Screen:** Untick and tick Hillshade and Optical (CTX 6 m). Drag one opacity slider.
3. **Say:** The slope layer shows ground that is too steep to cross.
   **Screen:** Tick Slope only. Drag its opacity up.
4. **Say:** Points of interest mark places worth a visit, and each one says why it was chosen.
   **Screen:** Click Cliff A. Point at Source: hillshade and the Evidence line.
5. **Say:** Every layer has a data card: source, version, license, and known limits.
   **Screen:** Click Data info on Slope. Pause on Known Issues.
6. **Say:** Even our own limits are on the screen.
   **Screen:** Keep the card open for two seconds.
7. **Say:** Martian Map. Mars data you can trace.
   **Screen:** Close the card. Hold on the full map and the status line.
